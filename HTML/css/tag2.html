About Ford Ranger Raptor

"Ford Ranger Raptor"

The Ford Ranger Raptor is a high-performance, factory-ready off-road truck developed by Ford Performance, featuring a reinforced frame, specialized FOX racing suspension, and aggressive styling. Built for high-speed desert running, it combines a powerful V6 twin-turbo engine with advanced four-wheel drive, making it a capable, street-legal racer.
